Website privacy

Effective November 6, 2026

The short version: this website has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. We don’t collect anything about you when you visit.

This website

This page covers warheadgaming.net, run by WarHead Gaming (“we”, “us”).

  • No cookies or tracking. The site doesn’t set cookies, use analytics or show ads.
  • No third-party scripts or fonts. Everything on these pages, including fonts and images, comes from this site.
  • Hosting logs. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. They use these to run and protect the service. We don’t use them to identify you.
  • Links to other sites. Links to Discord, itch.io, the Microsoft Store and our merch store take you to services with their own privacy policies.
  • Merch orders. Our merch store is run by Fourthwall. When you buy something there, Fourthwall handles your order, payment and shipping details under its own privacy policy.

If you email us

When you write to one of our addresses, we get your email address and what you send. We use it only to reply and keep it in our email account (Google Workspace). We don’t sell it, share it or add you to a mailing list. Ask us and we’ll delete your emails.

Children

We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the EU and UK GDPR let you ask what we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. We don’t sell or share personal information. Send any request to the address below and we’ll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
